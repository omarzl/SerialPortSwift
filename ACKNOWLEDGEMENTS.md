# Acknowledgements

SerialPortSwift is an independent Swift implementation. Its behaviour follows
[ORSSerialPort](https://github.com/armadsen/ORSSerialPort) by Andrew Madsen, which is released
under the MIT License. That covers how a port is opened and configured, how ports are discovered,
and how a reply is recognised among the bytes received. Thank you to its author and contributors
for years of a dependable serial port on the Mac.
